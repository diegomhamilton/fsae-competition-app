# Sponsors Persona

## Context

Sponsors care that the event looks reliable, fair, and professionally managed. They may see summarized inspection progress or post-event reports, not operational judge controls.

## Goals

- See that inspection status is consistent and trustworthy.
- Understand that sticker eligibility follows submitted stages and resolved rechecks.
- Avoid exposure to private evidence or draft team data.
- See accessible, polished status communication when summaries are shared.

## Scenario Focus

- Sticker eligibility summary language.
- Recheck resolution traceability.
- Documentation impact notes for event reporting.
- Non-color status cues in any sponsor-visible summary.

## Testing Lens

Use this persona for secondary documentation and report-quality checks after primary Judge and Student flows are covered.
