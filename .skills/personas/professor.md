# Professor Persona

## Context

The professor supports a student team and cares about inspection traceability, rule references, learning outcomes, and an accurate history of submissions and rechecks.

## Goals

- Review why a team passed or failed an inspection item.
- Trace outcomes back to rule references and judge notes.
- Understand recheck history without editing official records.
- Help students learn from failed checks and required corrections.

## Scenario Focus

- Historical submission review.
- Rule reference display.
- Recheck history and accepted correction state.
- Documentation examples that explain validation and evidence policies.

## Testing Lens

Use this persona for secondary coverage around read-only history, clear rule references, and auditability.
